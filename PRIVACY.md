# Privacy Policy: Markback – Timestamp Bookmarks for YouTube™

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

Markback does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

- The bookmarks you create (the video's ID, title, type, the bookmark times, and your notes).
- Your settings for the extension.

This is saved with Chrome's built-in storage (`chrome.storage.sync`) in your own browser. If you're signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs it to your other computers through your Google account. The developer has no access to it.

Preview frames of the video are captured on your computer only while the popup is open and are never saved or sent anywhere.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any other server.
- It does not use analytics, ads, or tracking.
- It does not read your browsing on any website other than YouTube.

## Deleting your data

Delete bookmarks from the extension's popup (including "Delete All Bookmarks"), or uninstall the extension to remove everything it saved.
